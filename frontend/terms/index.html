2. Intended Use & Scope

Kanakku is an open-source, non-commercial software application built for personal finance management. Its intended purpose includes:

  • Helping individuals track day-to-day income, expenses, and cash withdrawals across UPI, bank accounts, cards, and physical cash.
  • Providing local-first ledger storage directly inside the user's browser (IndexedDB).
  • Automating the entry of bank and payment confirmation alerts via optional, read-only Gmail synchronization.
  • Generating personal financial reports, spending category breakdowns, and exportable PDF summaries.
  • Offering conversational personal finance assistance via local deterministic computational tools and Google Gemini AI.

The Application is intended exclusively for personal, individual, non-commercial productivity use. It is not designed or licensed for enterprise banking operations, corporate payroll management, or audited institutional accounting.
